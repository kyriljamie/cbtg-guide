Questions

What happens after the first 3 months?

Your plan moves to its regular monthly price (Starter ₱599, Pro ₱999), and you can upgrade to a higher plan anytime. Before the 3 months end, we send you a report of the bookings you handled and the time you saved, so you can decide.

Can I see the app first?

Yes. Watch the demo video at the top of this page, or message us for a live walkthrough on Messenger video call.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime, and your bookings and settings carry over.

Do my staff need their own phones?

The staff app runs on any Android or iPhone. Several staff members can share one login per resort if you prefer.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya or bank transfer. Annual plans are paid once a year.

Will you help me set it up?

Yes. We add your units, rates and existing bookings for you. Elite plans get priority setup within 48 hours.
