Questions

Good to know

How do I reserve my date?

Message us your date and number of guests. We'll confirm availability and send payment details. A ₱2,000 reservation fee holds your date.

Is there a quiet time?

Not in Cabin 1. It's a sound-insulated glass cabin, kaya pwede ang videoke hanggang umaga.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Yes. Every cabin has complete kitchenware, free gasul and purified water, and there's no corkage fee.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM and check-out at 12:00 noon.

We're a big group. Can we book more than one cabin?

Yes. Cabins 1 + 2 fit up to 24 guests, and Cabins 2 + 3 up to 20. Message us for whole-property bookings.
