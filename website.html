Good to know

How do I reserve my dates?

Pick your dates above and send the request on Messenger or by text. We'll confirm availability and send the payment details. A reservation fee of 30% of your total holds your dates.

Why is booking direct cheaper than Airbnb?

Airbnb adds its own service fees. When you book with us directly, there's no middleman, so we pass the savings to you.

How many guests can we bring?

Charlie and Charlotte are comfortable for up to 14 guests each. If you're a group of 15 and want to save, you can book just one cabin, but the sleeping area will be tight. Charlotte + Choco fits up to 20, and Charlie + Charlotte up to 24. Each booking includes a set number of guests (8 for one cabin, 12 for Charlotte + Choco, 16 for Charlie + Charlotte). Extra guests are ₱600 each per night, or ₱750 for nights in December.

Are holiday rates different?

Yes. From December 12 to January 15, one cabin is ₱15,000 a night and two cabins are ₱20,000 a night. Holiday dates fill up first, so book early.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Yes. There's no corkage fee, and every cabin has complete kitchenware, free gasul and purified water.

Is there a quiet time?

Not in Charlie. It's a sound-insulated glass cabin, so videoke until morning is fine.

Are there pets on the property?

Our cats Charlotte and Choco live here in their own house. They don't go inside the cabins. If anyone in your group has allergies, let us know and we'll keep them away from your area.
