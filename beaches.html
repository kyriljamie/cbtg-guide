Before you go

Beach tips

  • Check the tide. The Pagbilao sandbars only show at low tide, usually mid to late afternoon. Ask your boatman the day before.
  • Ingat sa salabay. Box jellyfish show up in some months on Tayabas Bay. Follow the lifeguards, and bring vinegar and aqua shoes.
  • Bring cash. Entrance, cottage, tent and boat fees are mostly cash only, and signal is weak on the islands.
  • Pawikan nests. Sea turtles lay eggs at Costa Sariya in season. Keep clear of marked areas and bring your trash home.
